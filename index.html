Anwar Hossain Efat

MS student and Graduate Teaching Assistant
Department of Computer Science, Idaho State University

I study how people experience, build, and trust interactive and intelligent systems. My work sits between human–computer interaction and software engineering.

For my MS thesis, supervised by Dr. Farjana Z. Eishita, I am building a serious game for desktop, Android, and VR that delivers graded exposure therapy for germophobia and adapts to the player’s heart rate in real time. With Dr. Minhaz F. Zibran, I study how developers respond to pull requests written by AI coding agents. I also audit how health and wellness apps for VR headsets disclose and protect the sensitive data they collect, and I am scaling exam timetabling to a million students with parallel graph colouring.

Before coming to Idaho State, I was a Lecturer in Computer Science and Engineering at IUBAT in Dhaka, and my earlier research applied machine learning and explainable AI to medical diagnosis. I am looking for PhD opportunities in HCI and software engineering.
